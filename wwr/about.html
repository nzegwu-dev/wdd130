Wild River Rafters

Our mission is to give every guest a thrilling and safe river adventure.

History

Wild River Rafters began in 2010 when a small group of friends, all lifelong river lovers, decided to share the thrill of white water with the world. what started with two rafts and a borrowed trailer has grown into a team of expert guides serving thousands of guests every year. Safety and fun have been our top priorities from the very first trip.
